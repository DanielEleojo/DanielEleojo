### 👋 About Me

I'm a data scientist and app developer based in Ontario, building tools at the intersection of sustainability, AI, and mobile tech. Currently shipping **EcoCommute** — a B2B2C platform that rewards post-secondary students for walking and cycling to campus — and **NetworkAI**, a Flutter-based networking and CRM app with Firebase/Firestore under the hood.
